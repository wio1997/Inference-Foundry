## Limits (do not over-read)

- These are group counts in the captured scope; not whole-model or whole-device utilization.
- AIC and AIV ratios use different engines and different denominators; the two eligible lists
  must not be added (5,562) nor their ratios summed or averaged together.
- "Eligibility" means the engine's cycle and ratio columns were valid and positive for every
  task in the group; it is not a statement of achieved utilization.
- The overlap is structured by `Accelerator Core` (only MIX_AIC groups qualify for both engines);
  operator names containing "AiCore" can still be reported on AI_VECTOR_CORE rows, so engine
  must be read from the core column, not from the name.
