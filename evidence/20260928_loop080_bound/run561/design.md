## Fixed-`W₀` evidence and control

Save per-rank per-cycle acceptance/count arrays and logical Target/DSpark evaluations for the old cohort, selected output prefix/final equality, all8 state/KV/park mask and Graph buffer ownership across pause. A fixed-`W₀` Scheduling witness requires a matched no-publication control with the same initial request/state and matching acceptance/count/work. Numerical nondeterminism calls for same-state or A0/B/A1 controls and first-divergence diagnosis. If trajectories differ, the result may still certify API protocol and legal arrival, but cannot certify a fixed-`W₀` timing or feasible schedule. Record pause, all-reduce, D2H, RPC and Host overhead separately. These costs belong to the feasibility construction, not a zero-cost Scheduling Bound.

The private partial payload also needs a real RPC serializer/receiver CPU round-trip test. `unique_reply_rank=0` cannot substitute for all8 generation-bound acknowledgments, and the lazy continuation FutureWrapper must have one reader. `OutputProcessor` can finish a terminal `FinishReason.LENGTH` row without automatically aborting the Scheduler ghost, but the trial must explicitly detect and reject any unexpected ghost abort.

**No finite Resource/Hardware, Scheduling or Product endpoint changes.** After source preflight, CPU protocol/serializer negatives and independent Astra review, a guarded live B may test legality. A legal arrival alone still does not certify Scheduler admission, all8 concurrent residual-prefill/Target service or a formal E2E gain.
