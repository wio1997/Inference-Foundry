A strict lower endpoint can follow from one necessary path/cut subset; it need not reconstruct every rank and every edge if the selected subset and timed boundary are independently proved. ### Engineering node conflates witnessed performance with a predictive resource model

`engineering_attainable_endpoint` requires attainable_node_service as well as a legal schedule and formal_correct_repeats. That is appropriate for the inherited endpoint type `repeated_formal_feasibility_plus_predictive_model`. It is stronger than necessary for a directly witnessed repeated formal performance point: measured correct E2E can establish that point without separately measuring every compute/HBM/HCCL service. Keep the node restricted to the modeled endpoint, or add a separate witnessed-formal-point node. Neither a measured point nor a finite sample range alone proves a universal optimum/confidence interval.

### Future promotion still depends on trusted human attestation and a separate numeric checker

`validate_dag` validates exact contract-string equality, dependency existence, acyclicity and status propagation. It does not inspect evidence files/hashes, missing lists, status enums, kind compatibility, or physical numeric quantities. An attestor can mark a node certified while its missing list remains populated; that is not rejected. Identical contract strings do not establish an actual scope join. The two positive mutation tests above intentionally illustrate this boundary.

The promotion_rule explicitly reserves positive finite values/units to an independent check, but no such checker exists here. Before numeric output is allowed, require a machine-readable W/C or path payload, conservative uncertainty and scope/rank aggregation checks, immutable source evidence identifiers, and an independently signed/resolved attestation record. Do not interpret `certified=True` alone as a computed valid numerical Bound.
