## Minimum certificates and logical corrections

**Strict outer ceiling.** For fixed measured output count N, prove T ≥ W−/C+ and TPS ≤ N·C+/W−. The certificate must state legal implementation class, required consumer, fresh work inside the measured interval, precision/operation convention, all allowed engines, exact hardware applicability and conservative maximum-rate envelope. One positive subset suffices; complete Draft/KV/communication/scheduling coverage is unnecessary.

Run423's BF16 wo_a projection remains the smallest well-described candidate. Run431 strengthens identity to Wuzhou S900K3 / IT21HMDC_Bin6 / 910B3 but supplies no matching maximum BF16 rate/issue and clock applicability. Neither measured GEMM throughput nor observed 1800 MHz supplies C+. Unrelated Atlas FP16 rates cannot simply be assigned to BF16 or W4A8 counts. Finite experiments can refute a cap; slower samples cannot prove it universal.

**Reduce the freshness obligation where possible.** When the conclusion concerns whole-product TPS, select the whole benchmark start/end boundary. Work before Runtime handoff but after benchmark start may belong to W−. Exact D(H_run), publication and all-token handoff accounting are required only when the chosen numerator subtracts them, counts every new retained Runtime row, or models that cut. They are not prerequisites for proving one required projection in the full measured window. Warmup/precomputed reusable work must still be excluded; Run427 counts cannot transfer to Run99.

**Algorithm class cannot be settled by more current traces.** Perfect row observation establishes Current work, not that every legal implementation performs conventional dense 2MNK. Reuse, factorization, representation changes and permitted output caching must be covered or explicitly excluded from the named class. A conventional-dense certificate can be useful conditionally; it must not silently redefine the broader product optimization freedom.

**Scheduling minimum.** A selected necessary path with valid node-duration lower bounds and the correct window can establish a strict relaxation without the complete all-eight-rank DAG. Include every relevant rank/storage edge for that selected claim. Measured service times are attained durations, not unavoidable minima. Host enqueue order is not device finish-to-start; max(rank-local elapsed duration) is not a common-clock makespan. Resource floors cannot be added without required seriality, and a simple maximum can be too weak when a proved dependency forces serial work.

**Run437 does not automatically close all43 or W−.** Final preflight explicitly retains all43 CONDITIONAL_NATIVE and external retention OPEN. Route/group parity, FULL kwargs→state storage and CP prefix checks improve the current numerator. Per-layer branch/layer→CP composition, all-slot native semantics, external retention, unavoidable work and capacity remain separate obligations.

### 2. One-projection W− plus exact-board C+ certificate

**Target:** strict Hardware/Resource outer ceiling. Obtain authoritative maximum BF16 rate or issue-width × maximum-clock envelope applicable to the installed board/bin, with engine coverage and operation convention. This is primarily documentary/configuration evidence, not another GEMM run. Join one required fresh projection to its retained-result consumer and measured-window lineage under an explicit conventional-dense class. Whole-model row propagation is unnecessary if that lineage is independently proved.

**Acceptance:** positive compatible W−/C+, evidence hashes, units, legal class, timed window, aggregation and conservative uncertainty. Compute the ceiling only after both clauses pass. Leave it open if applicability or compulsory membership remains unproved; no arbitrary multiplier or measured peak substitute.

**Expected contraction:** potentially unbounded→finite for the strict endpoint; likely weak practical contraction if the subset is tiny. This is the smallest certificate for first finiteness. Once it exists, further independent work subsets can tighten the maximum of valid resource floors without being initial prerequisites.

## Proof DAG gate

Run435/436's 17-node all-open DAG is safe today. Its validator establishes structure/status propagation, not evidence truth or numerical sufficiency. Before numeric promotion require a typed W/C or selected-path payload, immutable evidence identity, units, class/window joins, aggregation and uncertainty checks.
