## Bound structure and uncertainty to reduce

For a declared execution class, maintain separate objects:

1. **Resource relaxation:** necessary work/traffic constraints. A cumulative service guarantee W≤C+T+B gives T≥max(0,W−−B)/C+. HBM/network terms require their own units, storage/recomputation/placement assumptions and capacity envelopes. Attained rates are not C+.
2. **Necessary scheduling relaxation:** a semantic DAG with only proved data/lifetime/arrival dependencies, node service lower bounds and shared-resource constraints. The longest necessary path and resource-load relaxations can jointly strengthen the time floor. Do not sum unrelated lower bounds or retain implementation-only serialization by default.
3. **Feasible execution estimate:** one coherent schedule whose resource sharing, storage, collectives and client release rules are satisfied, calibrated by compatible attained service measurements. A costed schedule is conditional until its overlap/contention and execution semantics are validated. Optimistic independent node costs do not establish attainability.
4. **Product interval:** full request release through external completion, including pre-window state/reuse rules, residual prefill/seed, state/KV, acceptance and drain. A rigorously certified time floor L would imply a TPS ceiling49152/L. A demonstrated complete execution supplies an achieved point; statistical formal medians are not deterministic guarantees. Current571.681 is the retained formal reference.

Keep conditional Engineering estimates visible with assumptions/confidence even while strict Hardware endpoints are null. Exact-board C+ absence does not logically prohibit calibrating an engineering schedule. Conversely a fitted engineering estimate is not a hardware ceiling. An all-null proof ledger should not become a reason to pursue only byte-level identity indefinitely.
