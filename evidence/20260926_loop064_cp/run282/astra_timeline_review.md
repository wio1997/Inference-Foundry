## Resource-contention challenge

Independent historical Run246 comparison finds a large simultaneous change in the overlapped main-stream Rotary task. Across16 rank-cycle samples, latest Run246 first-c4 Rotary median is5.640us, versus64.190us in Run282. Rotary-start to QLI-start changes43.5 ->105.125us. QLI medians remain83.922 ->82.512us, and subsequent sparse attention44.231 ->44.041us. The nearby indexer-query quant-matmul start to sparse-attention start is246.625 ->208.750us.

These are source-order aligned cross-run synchronized-profiler observations, not a matched causal experiment. Historical memory-counter profiling and present profiling may perturb durations differently. Nevertheless, the ~59us Rotary expansion is an important warning: ~65us of overlapping task intervals is not ~65us of wall saving. Resource contention, kernel occupancy or task-duration accounting could explain the apparent concurrency. The local envelope still appears shorter by~38us in this historical screen, so neither zero gain nor a positive gain is proven. Full rows/trace paths are in astra_rotary_contention_screen.json; no kernel sum is promoted to an E2E result.

The conditional no-contention bound remains delta <= min(serial indexer branch span, serial compressor+metadata+scatter branch span), with both spans measured on the same path and state. A measured overlap interval is not this bound. Do not count the entire QLI duration as hidden, omit branch metadata/scatter, or multiply one-layer overlap by21.

## Addendum: matched immediate/overlap discrimination

An additional independent read of Run260 latest eight-rank traces confirms the same first-c4 Rotary baseline scale:16 rank-cycle samples, median5.670us, range5.440-6.141us. This corroborates the historical Run246 screen but still does not remove differing-profile/run confounding.

For the next immediate versus overlap profile, preserve the exact profiler mode, synchronization, counter settings, graph shape, selected layer, state/position regime and source except the wait placement. Record independent capture identities and full handoff coverage. Observe: fork-entry through both branches' completion; CompressorMetadata/Compressor/scatter service durations and gaps; indexer quant matmul/Rotary/Hadamard/weights/quant/QLI durations; aux event record and main wait; sparse-attention and output-projection completion; per-rank next-collective arrival and complete Target/cycle. Use an explicit shared boundary for the two schedules, not independently chosen nearest tasks. Join location and next-consumer readiness matter more than the overlapping rectangles.

Decision logic for Sol: (1) correctness and matching execution coverage first; (2) if overlap is present but Rotary or another main task elongates, quantify full fork-to-consumer wall rather than subtracting that elongation or overlap from task sums; (3) an absent or adverse local wall delta rejects promotion of this particular allocation even if aux work is fully overlapped; (4) a reproducible positive local delta justifies the next all-layer/cycle test, not a linear21x E2E claim; (5) A0 remains necessary because immediate already changes original order. If measurement uncertainty is larger than the delta, use bounded same-state repeat samples rather than inventing a performance verdict from two profiler windows.

The bound implication is structural: independent semantic branches can compete for mixed AIC/AIV execution capacity and HBM. A scalar per-node cost taken in isolation does not remain constant under concurrency. Resource allocation/interference must be attached to the schedule, or represented as a measured feasible service envelope for co-runners. Separate Cube and Vector maxima with free simultaneous use would be optimistic unless the particular910B3 kernel allocation supports it. This experiment can calibrate that envelope, but does not close alternative start times, stream allocation, kernel block allocation, local-Q/hidden-gather overlap, qr fan-out or broader execution architectures. Changing block allocation or delaying launch to another phase is a future hypothesis requiring its own evidence, not an assumed solution.
