The only downstream page intersections are from a deliberately conservative **full historical prefix** of the layer3/4/5 Compressor state caches: 1,422 / 638 / 1,034 hit records across eight ranks and ten samples. The closest hit pages end at least **1126 / 222 / 1326 original tokens before** those readers' current `start_pos`; respectively 174 / 109 / 181 hits belong to each rank's two owner request slots. The rest concern nonowner requests, whose producer work may itself be removable in an all-layer ownership design. No layer0/1 previous-sample-cycle read hit appears.

These hits are possible reuse of old physical pages within a shared backing, **not observed native state reads**. Their age makes the c4/c128 recurrent live-window question decisive. A `state_cache` table ratio of 1 says how positions address pages; it does not say whether the producer reads one, eight, 128 or another number of previous state tokens. The current source's full-prefix envelope is safe for exclusion when disjoint, but a hit cannot establish semantic RAW or mathematical necessity. Page epochs, values, last writer, exact native address set and longer continuous lifetimes remain unproved. Do not promote a compulsory traffic amount, a Scheduling Bound or a Product TPS bound.
