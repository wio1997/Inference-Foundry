Event spans include possible Python/Host submission gaps, and the prestate reset perturbs cache residency; they are neither pure kernel capacity nor FULL Graph cost. They do not disprove the correctness-preserving owner mechanism or a broader DSA producer architecture. The diagnostic 83.473 tok/s carrier is instrumentation overhead, not a Product E2E result.
