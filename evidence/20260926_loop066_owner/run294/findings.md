The one-shot fixture ran at the actual eager Target Runtime entry on all eight ranks. Shared prefix/page aliases or extra full96 writes may explain the difference, but the first differing physical byte and writer have not been measured. Candidate semantics remain **unproved**; no owner-update traffic is classified as removable.

Target was deliberately eager (`target_graph_mode=NONE`), and the four extra private native calls plus full 141.8MB/rank storage clones make its 83.299 tok/s diagnostic rate unusable for Product comparison.
