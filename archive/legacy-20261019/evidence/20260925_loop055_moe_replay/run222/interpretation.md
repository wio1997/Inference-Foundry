# Run222 bank admission and hidden-input audit

Run204 observed53 prefill forwards across seven diagnostic cohorts, each with exactly one first88 forward. The frozen model has43 decoder layers and source instantiates DeepseekV4MoE in every layer. At Run221 2.92-3.11ms per one MoE call, full 43-layer first88 coverage suggests an optimistic126-134ms/cohort local exposure; all seven to nine forwards would screen near0.9-1.2s/cohort. These are extrapolations, not measured whole-prefill savings.

Training on Run184/186/188_A, the three most common exact (tokens,requests) signatures cover only1-3 calls in each of four held-out cohorts. Even a 12-entry exact-signature bank covers only3-4. A token-count-only bank could have fewer entries, but it is not yet legal: the first three hash-routing layers read forward_context.input_ids through experts_selector even though the MoE custom-op input_ids argument is null. The buffer source supports current-token refresh at a stable device allocation, but Run219/221 did not record actual input-ID pointer/value under graph replay. Request-count and DP/SP dependence must be established before reducing the bank key.

Graph capture in Run221 was about50-60ms per rank for one layer; at roughly3ms local saving, an entry needs about17-21 reuses to amortize capture before integration costs. The first88 entry appears once in each traced cohort, while formal warmup coverage and long-running production reuse are unmeasured. Output ownership across multiple in-flight replays is also unproven.

Decision: proceed to a bounded live ABI and input-ID refresh probe covering the first three hash layers and one non-hash layer on distinct real prefill signatures, plus per-forward call frequency. Do not create a broad bank or claim an E2E gain from Run221 arithmetic. An independent Astra High review was requested; the backend model identity is not independently verifiable from available execution records. Its read-only conclusions informed this audit; Sol retains the decision.
