## Decision

**No currently available small benchmark can certify exact-board C_plus/B, and no existing V3.40 row/task/traffic count is a strict architecture-wide W_minus.** Confidence: high. The smallest useful new acquisition is a **semantic freshness/entry-credit addendum to the already justified typed-KV two-cycle packet**, not another standalone one-row Run585-style service, traffic counter pass, or peak benchmark.

This addendum can certify a nonzero necessary arithmetic subset **within an explicitly declared online fixed-expression evaluation class**, if its gates pass. It cannot by observation alone establish an unrestricted algebraic lower bound. If the desired endpoint must cover every mathematically equivalent algorithm allowed by Extreme, retain W_minus=null until a separate complexity/representation argument closes that broader class.

A positive partial numerator alone is not a positive time floor: (W_minus-B) must also be positive and the matching C_plus finite. A single projection may be swallowed by the eventual burst allowance. Its value is to validate a reusable proof schema, then scale only over proven semantic keys.

Initial prefix/KV/results and proven reuse are credited. Fusion, layout, persistent execution, recomputation and scheduling remain free. This class is an analytical column, not a restriction on Extreme's development.

**Admissible result:** one or more distinct required fresh contraction instances in the declared class, with explicit entry credit. For a standard dense K-by-D contraction, report K*D multiply terms and D*(K-1) additions, or labelled conventional2*K*D operation equivalents when paired with a matching MAC convention. Do not assume BF16 from the method name: actual quant method may require a separate resource unit. This is a source/semantic necessity certificate under the class, not a theorem that every numerically equivalent algorithm must perform that many physical FLOPs. Fusion may remove its output materialization; consequently **no positive compulsory HBM-byte count is certified by this witness alone**; the necessary count remains unknown, not zero. No current cache write or later read is automatically an HBM crossing.

Copy after producer/before overwrite on the existing ordering, export after existing drain; no new synchronization, model call, HCCL or acceptance change. Diagnostic observer cost is nonzero. No timing claim is made, so an A0/A1 service comparison is unnecessary for this identity gate; any later duration transfer requires observer controls.

## Exact-board C_plus/B: precise remaining acquisition

The smallest potentially decisive capacity acquisition is **one authoritative applicable maximum-envelope document/configuration certificate**, not another load test. Reuse Run462 and Run590 identity and exclusions; do not repeat their inaccessible public/OEM/API searches without new access or evidence.

For the exact S900K3 /910B3 IT21HMDC_Bin6,20Cube+40Vector, architecture2201, driver26.0.rc1/firmware7.5.0.6.220/CANN9.1.0 configuration, obtain:

- Compute: maximum enabled issue capacity per relevant precision/engine and maximum allowed clock including boost/tolerance; applicable engines and simultaneous constraints. A compute-class formula such as8192 conventional BF16 ops/Cube-cycle times20 times f_max is only symbolic until its applicability is certified.
- HBM: active physical channels/bus, maximum transfer clock/tolerance and payload/raw-unit relationship. Capacity64GiB and sampled1600MHz do not supply this.
- HCCS: directed port-peer/lane graph including alternate routes and maximum serializer rate/tolerance. Standard224Gb/s observations and library busbw do not certify an all-interval cut cap.
- For each resource, an explicit service counting convention and burst/in-flight allowance B. If counting completions, bound work already in flight at the interval start; if counting issues/transfers, align W_minus to the same convention and account for boundary granularity. Do not set B=0 or derive it from the500ms HCCS cache timer.

A conservative upper envelope is acceptable; it need not be attained. It must cover all permitted engines/paths or carry an explicit restricted-class label. The installed source→device image→booted implementation and supported API/freshness joins remain necessary where the claim relies on implementation rather than an architecture-wide physical cap.

Run462 already found public supported management queries insufficient and OEM download authentication unavailable. No new permission or contact was requested or used in this review. That certificate path is presently blocked on new authoritative information; it does not block the proposed combined identity packet or conditional Engineering calibration.
